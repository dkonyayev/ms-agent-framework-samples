# Meridian Flow — Pricing Plans

Meridian Flow offers four subscription tiers, billed per user per month (annual
billing saves 20%).

| Plan | Price (monthly, per user) | Seat minimum | Key limits |
|------|---------------------------|---------------|------------|
| Starter | $8 | 1 | 3 active projects, 5 automations |
| Team | $15 | 5 | Unlimited projects, 50 automations |
| Business | $29 | 10 | Unlimited automations, REST API access, SSO |
| Enterprise | Custom (contact sales) | 50 | Dedicated support manager, audit logs, custom data retention |

## Trial and refunds

New accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan, no credit card
required. Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within the first 30 days
of the billing cycle; after 30 days, refunds are prorated for the unused months
remaining in the term.

## Nonprofit and education discounts

Verified nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off the
Team and Business plans. Discounts are applied after verification through the
billing portal and are not retroactive to prior invoices.
