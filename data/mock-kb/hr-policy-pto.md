# Meridian Cloudworks — Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per calendar year, front-loaded in
full on January 1st (new hires receive a prorated amount based on start date).
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the following year; any remaining
unused days above that are forfeited on December 31st, except where prohibited
by local law.

## New hire waiting period

New employees may begin using accrued PTO immediately — there is no waiting
period.

## Holidays

Meridian Cloudworks observes 10 paid company holidays per year, published
annually by People Operations in December for the following year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days
per year, which do not carry over and are not paid out upon termination.

## Requesting time off

PTO requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance for requests longer than 2 days. Manager approval is required for all
PTO requests.
