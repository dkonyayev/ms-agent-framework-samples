# Meridian Flow Support — Troubleshooting FAQ

**Q: Boards are loading slowly or not at all. What should I do?**
A: First, clear your browser cache and confirm you're on a supported browser
version (latest two major versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari). If the
issue persists, check status.meridiancloudworks.example for an ongoing
incident before contacting support.

**Q: Automations aren't triggering.**
A: Automations pause automatically after 10 consecutive failures to prevent
runaway notifications. Go to Automations → [rule name] → Resume to
re-enable a paused rule, then check the rule's trigger condition for typos.

**Q: I'm not receiving email notifications.**
A: Check Settings → Notifications to confirm email alerts are enabled for the
relevant project. Also check your spam folder for messages from
notifications@meridiancloudworks.example, and confirm your organization's email
allowlist includes that domain.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Use the in-app "Report a Problem" button (bottom-right of any screen), or
email support@meridiancloudworks.example with a description and screenshot.

**Q: What's the support SLA?**
A: Starter and Team plans receive a 2-business-day first response via email.
Business plans get 1-business-day first response via chat or email. Enterprise
plans get a 4-hour first response, 24/7, via their dedicated support manager.
