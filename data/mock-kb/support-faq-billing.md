# Meridian Flow Support — Billing FAQ

**Q: How do I change my plan?**
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and
are billed on a prorated basis for the remainder of the current cycle.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Visa, Mastercard, and American Express credit cards, plus ACH bank transfer
for Business and Enterprise plans invoiced annually.

**Q: Why was my card declined?**
A: The most common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or the
issuing bank flagging the charge as suspicious. Update your payment method in
Settings → Billing → Payment Methods, or contact your bank.

**Q: Can I get an invoice instead of being charged automatically?**
A: Yes, but only on annual Business and Enterprise plans. Contact
billing@meridiancloudworks.example to switch to invoiced billing.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your workspace remains
accessible in read-only mode for 30 days after cancellation, after which data
is permanently deleted.
