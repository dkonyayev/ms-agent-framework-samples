# Meridian Cloudworks — Remote Work Policy

Meridian Cloudworks operates as a remote-first company. Employees may work from
any location within their country of employment without prior approval.
Working from a different country for more than 30 consecutive days requires
written approval from People Operations, due to tax and compliance
requirements.

## Home office stipend

Full-time employees receive a one-time $1,000 home office setup stipend during
their first 60 days, plus an ongoing $50/month stipend toward internet and
utilities. Stipend requests are submitted through the internal Expenses portal.

## Core collaboration hours

Employees are expected to be available for meetings between 11:00 AM and 3:00
PM in their team's designated primary time zone, Monday through Thursday.
Fridays have no mandated core hours.

## Co-working space allowance

Employees who do not wish to work from home may expense up to $300/month for a
shared co-working space membership, with manager approval.
